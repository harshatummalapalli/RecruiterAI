Summary

We are seeking a highly skilled Staff Software Engineer/ Solution Architect - AI Solutions with strong expertise in Full Stack Software Development, Solution Design, Data Engineering, and AI/ML implementation.

This is a hands-on engineering role for an experienced software developer who combines strong technical design skills with recent experience building and deploying AI-powered applications. The ideal candidate will be responsible for designing scalable solutions, contributing to software development, and implementing modern AI capabilities that align with our product and solution offerings.

As a Staff Software Engineer/ Solution Architect, you will work closely with business stakeholders, Product Owners, leadership teams, clients, data scientists, consultants, DevOps engineers, and software engineers to design, build, and maintain enterprise-grade applications and AI-driven solutions. You will play a key role in translating complex business problems into scalable technical solutions while ensuring engineering excellence, maintainability, and long-term product success.

Partner with business and product stakeholders to understand requirements, design end-to-end solutions, and help Product Owners break down initiatives into actionable user stories and technical deliverables.

Integrate and optimize data from various internal and external systems, understanding the challenges associated with enterprise data integration across SaaS applications.
Design, develop, and maintain APIs, services, and integrations that support enterprise applications and AI solutions.
Evaluate and adopt emerging technologies to improve scalability, maintainability, performance, and reliability of applications and AI systems.
Collaborate with data engineers and data scientists to ensure data quality, availability, and successful deployment of AI and machine learning capabilities.
Design, implement, and maintain data pipelines, processing frameworks, and supporting infrastructure required for AI and machine learning workloads.
Build and deploy AI-powered applications utilizing Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Agentic AI frameworks, and modern AI orchestration patterns.

Communicate technical solutions, risks, and recommendations effectively to leadership, business stakeholders, and clients.

Provide technical leadership, mentor team members, resolve complex technical challenges, and foster a collaborative engineering culture.
