Senior Manager – FP&A and Business Finance

Location: Hyderabad, India
Work Mode: Hybrid
Experience: 8–12 years

Job Summary

We are looking for a Senior Manager – FP&A and Business Finance to lead financial planning, forecasting, performance analysis, and business decision support for a large, complex organization. The role will partner closely with business leaders to translate financial information into actionable insights, improve forecasting accuracy, and support strategic and operational decisions.

The successful candidate will combine strong financial analysis and modeling capabilities with commercial judgment, stakeholder management, and the ability to communicate financial insights clearly to senior business leaders.

Key Responsibilities

- Lead the annual budgeting, forecasting, and long-range planning processes for assigned business areas.
- Develop and maintain financial models to support planning, scenario analysis, investment decisions, and business performance reviews.
- Analyze actuals versus budget and forecast, identify key drivers of variance, and provide recommendations to business leaders.
- Prepare monthly and quarterly management reporting packs with clear commentary on business performance.
- Partner with business and functional leaders to evaluate financial implications of strategic and operational initiatives.
- Develop scenario analyses and sensitivity models for revenue, cost, headcount, and investment decisions.
- Improve financial planning and reporting processes through automation, standardization, and data-driven analysis.
- Present financial insights and recommendations to senior leadership.
- Work cross-functionally with Accounting, Commercial, Operations, and other business teams.
- Support ad hoc financial analysis and strategic projects as required.

Qualifications

- Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or a related discipline.
- 8–12 years of relevant experience in FP&A, Business Finance, Commercial Finance, or closely related financial planning roles.
- Strong experience in financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, and variance analysis.
- Strong financial modeling skills, including scenario and sensitivity analysis.
- Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel.
- Working knowledge of Power BI or a similar business intelligence/reporting platform.
- Experience presenting financial analysis and recommendations to senior stakeholders.
- Strong business partnering and communication skills.
- Experience working with ERP systems such as SAP, Oracle, or comparable platforms.
- Professional qualification such as CA, CMA, ACCA, or MBA Finance is preferred.

Preferred Background

Experience in large multinational or complex enterprise environments is preferred.

Candidates with experience in organizations such as Unilever, Procter & Gamble, PepsiCo, Nestlé, Coca-Cola, Mondelez International, or similar large consumer/industrial organizations would be particularly relevant.

Experience supporting commercial, operations, or business-unit leadership is preferred.

Important Profile Consideration

This role requires meaningful FP&A/business-finance ownership. Candidates whose experience is exclusively in statutory audit, tax, bookkeeping, or transaction processing, without substantive FP&A or business-finance responsibilities, are not suitable.
